  • Set the hourly trigger

    10 minno reminders without it
    Why and how

    Create a free scheduler job (for example cron-job.org) that calls this every hour, after the hosting step:

    GET <your-app-url>/api/cron/reminders
    Authorization: Bearer <CRON_SECRET>

    Settings shows when it last ran. If it goes quiet for three hours, the app shows a banner, but not in the first 24 hours of an account: turn on the scheduler's own failure alerts as well.
